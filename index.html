Engineering Philosophy

Whether fine-tuning open-source LLMs or building scalable mobile applications, the core tenet remains the same: System Integrity. A model is only as good as its pipeline, and an app is only as reliable as its edge-case handling.

🔍

1. Eradicating Train-Serving Skew

Over-optimized classification reports are often a mirage. Ensuring feature extraction runs identically in development and production is non-negotiable.

🔒

2. Frictionless Security

Apps like Q-In require strict device identity validation without compromising user experience, especially during app reinstallation and account recovery.

📜

3. Explainability over Black Boxes

In legal tech, a prediction without a reason is dangerous. Implementing tools like SHAP ensures every IPC prediction can be audited by human experts.
